## 🔍 How Local Ranking Works (the 3 factors)

Google ranks local results mainly on three things — every recommendation in this plan maps back to one of them:

1. **Relevance** — how well the profile matches the search (categories, description, services, products).
2. **Distance** — how close the business is to the searcher (accurate address + map pin).
3. **Prominence** — how well-known and trusted the business is (reviews, ratings, photos, citations, website).

---

## ⚡ Summary of Key Findings

- **Profile completeness ≈ 55%** — missing secondary categories, products, attributes, and a proper description.
- **Only 1 category** ("Bakery") — losing visibility for "café", "cake shop", "dessert shop" searches.
- **Low review velocity** — reviews arrive irregularly and most are unanswered.
- **Few, outdated photos** — no interior, menu, or team photos.
- **No Google Posts / Q&A** — no fresh activity signals.
- **NAP inconsistency** — name/phone formats differ across Justdial, Zomato, and Instagram.

## ✅ Top 5 Priority Actions

1. Add secondary categories: **Cake shop, Café, Dessert shop, Pastry shop**.
2. Publish the optimized **750-character description** (keyword-rich, no stuffing).
3. Start a **QR-code + WhatsApp review request** flow; reply to every review within 48 hours.
4. Upload **20+ real photos** and post a **Google Post weekly**.
5. Fix **NAP consistency** across all directories and the website.

## 🗓️ 30-Day Implementation Roadmap

## 📊 KPIs to Track (GBP Performance tab)

- Search & Maps impressions
- Website clicks, calls, direction requests
- Number of reviews / average rating / response rate
- Photo views vs. competitors

---

## 🧠 Key Learnings

- A Google Business Profile is often the **first impression** of a local business — more than its website.
- **Primary category** is the single strongest relevance signal.
- **Genuine reviews + owner replies** build both ranking and customer trust; fake or incentivised reviews violate Google's policies.
- **Consistency** (same name, address, phone everywhere) quietly matters a lot.
- Local SEO is **ongoing**, not one-time — fresh posts, photos and reviews keep a profile competitive.
